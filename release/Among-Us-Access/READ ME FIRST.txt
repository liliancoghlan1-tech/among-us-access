====================================================================
 AMONG US ACCESS  -  NVDA accessibility mod for Among Us
 How to install  (the short version)
====================================================================

Everything you need is already in this folder - the mod AND the
framework it runs on. You do NOT download anything else.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
 TO INSTALL
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Make sure Among Us is CLOSED.

  2. Run "Install" (the file called Install.bat in this folder).
     A black window opens and does the work for you: it finds your
     Among Us folder automatically and copies the mod in.

     - If it cannot find the game, it will ask you to paste the
       folder path. In Steam: right click Among Us > Manage >
       Browse local files, and copy the path from the address bar.

     - When it says "DONE!", close the window.

  3. Start NVDA (or your screen reader).

  4. Launch Among Us from Steam. A few seconds after it opens you
     should hear:  "Among Us Accessibility Mod loaded"

That's it. If you hear that line, you're ready to play.

  * The FIRST launch may take a few extra seconds while the framework
    warms up. Every launch after that is quick.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
 TO REMOVE IT LATER
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Run "Uninstall" (Uninstall.bat) in this folder. It puts the game
back exactly as it was - nothing about Among Us itself is ever
changed.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
 WANT THE FULL GUIDE?
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Open "README.txt" in this folder. It explains every feature, every
key, and how to play each task by ear. It's long, but there's a quick
key reference near the bottom, and you can press H in a round any time
to hear your status.

Have fun, and good luck finding the impostor (or being one).
